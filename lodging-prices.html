Springdale is still the best Narrows base, but it is not the only available area. The expanded search includes Virgin, Apple Valley, La Verkin, Hurricane, Hildale, Colorado City, Mt. Where is the Zion shuttle? The main Zion Canyon park shuttle boards at the Zion Canyon Visitor Center in Springdale, immediately inside the South Entrance. It is different from the separate Springdale town shuttle. Every drive time below is an estimate to the Visitor Center/park-shuttle boarding area.
